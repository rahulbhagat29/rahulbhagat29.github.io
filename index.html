LendingClub Loan Risk & Portfolio Profit Analysis

Finance / Lending  |  2026

Decision: Identified 15% default rate as the profit-maximising approval threshold — below it, approvals erode margin; above it, viable loans are rejected unnecessarily. Risk-based pricing at 1.5x makes Grade C/D/E High DTI segments profitable. Grade F/G remain loss-making regardless of pricing and should be excluded from any approval policy.

Problem: Portfolio had no quantified approval policy — approving broadly increased defaults, approving conservatively left profitable loans rejected. No threshold existed to define where approval becomes profit-negative.

What I did: Cleaned 2.26M rows (2007–2018) in Python and Pandas, ran multi-segment SQL analysis using a three-CTE master query across risk grades and DTI bands, and built a Python policy simulation to identify the 15% threshold and model risk-based pricing scenarios across borrower segments.

Corporate Budget Variance Analysis & Stress Test Model

Finance / Corporate  |  2026

Decision: Product B and Operations together drive 80% of adverse variance — enabling targeted corrective action over broad cost cuts. Stress test showed a 58.7% Net P&L erosion (₹765L to ₹316L) under a −12% revenue and +8% cost scenario, giving management a quantified threshold for contingency planning.

Problem: MidCore Industries had no stress-tested view of P&L exposure — management could not quantify how much margin erosion a combined revenue shortfall and cost overrun would produce, making budget defence and scenario planning guesswork.

What I did: Built a 4-sheet financial model covering FY2023–FY2024 across 8 line items and 192 data rows with 2,264 formulas, zero errors. Engineered a Variance Engine with RAG status flags and a 6×5 sensitivity table to stress test Net P&L across revenue and cost combinations.
